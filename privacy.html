Veristrux Inc. builds local-first developer tools with Zero Content Telemetry. We never sell personal data, run third-party ad trackers, or upload your source code, AI prompts, chat transcripts, or local file paths to Veristrux servers.

1. Local-First Tools & Pro Licensing Data (What Leaves Your Device)

  • Offline / Zero-Network Tools: Iron Veil, UE5 Build & VRAM Doctor, UE 5.9 Cinema Capture Toolkit, Hybrid Silicon AI Tuner, ViewDrift (/ppd), and Token Czar Lite run locally without transmitting code, prompts, transcripts, or file paths to Veristrux.
  • Token Czar Pro License Check-In: While a paid Token Czar Pro subscription is active, the app contacts veristrux-tokenczar-stream.veristrux.workers.dev (hosted on Cloudflare Workers) at activation and approximately every 24 hours to validate seat entitlement and refresh its 72-hour Ed25519 lease, transmitting: (1) your license key, (2) a salted SHA-256 hardware seat hash (HWID, treated as pseudonymous device data under applicable privacy laws), (3) your activation_id, (4) Python/OS ABI string (e.g., cp312-win_amd64), (5) app version and EULA acceptance version, and (6) the SHA-256 hash of your installed Pro binary. Cloudflare necessarily processes your IP address to route HTTPS requests. We pass your license key and truncated HWID hash to Polar Software Inc. (Polar.sh) to enforce seat limits.
  • Activation & Fraud-Prevention Ledger (24-Month Retention): We retain a minimal activation ledger (UTC timestamp, activation_id, HWID hash, masked key, IP address, app version, and EULA version) in Cloudflare Workers KV with automatic twenty-four (24) month TTL expiration strictly for seat enforcement, abuse/HWID blocklisting on resolved payment disputes, and payment-dispute evidence (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) contract performance & Art. 6(1)(f) legitimate interest). No source code, prompts, transcripts, or file paths leave your machine.

2. Website, Hosting & Payment Processors

  • Static Storefront & Do Not Track (CalOPPA): Our storefront is hosted on GitHub Pages (which processes standard web-server access logs including IP addresses) and sets zero third-party marketing cookies or cross-site behavioral trackers. Because we do not track users across third-party sites, browser “Do Not Track” (DNT) signals do not alter our already zero-tracking behavior.
  • Payment & Billing Processors: Orders and subscriptions are processed by Polar Software Inc. (Polar.sh / Stripe), PayPal, itch.io, or Epic Games Fab. Veristrux never sees or stores raw payment card numbers and receives only order metadata (buyer email, order ID, SKU, subscription status) needed for fulfillment and support. Support emails to argoness@veristrux.com are hosted on Google Workspace.

3. Async Engineering Rescue Materials

Sanitized logs, crash dumps, or repro snippets submitted to argoness@veristrux.com for a fixed-scope engineering rescue are kept confidential, used solely to perform your engagement, and automatically deleted within thirty (30) days after engagement completion.
